From wish to goal – and to the first step

“More sport”, “less stress”, “finally that project” – those are wishes. A goal has a measure, a date and a reason that still holds on bad days. This tool checks your goal step by step: Is it a toward-goal or an away-from goal? Do you know how you measure success? Is the horizon realistic? Then you build milestones, if-then plans for the obstacles and the habit that carries the goal.
