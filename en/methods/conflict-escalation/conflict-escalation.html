Where does your conflict stand – and what helps now?

Friedrich Glasl describes nine stages on which conflicts escalate – from “hardening” to “together into the abyss”. Each stage has typical symptoms and needs a different response. Here you determine the stage, see your own part and choose a strategy that fits the stage.
