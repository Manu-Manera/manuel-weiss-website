40% of your happiness is in your hands

About half of your happiness level is innate, only 10% depends on circumstances – but 40% you decide through what you think and do each day. This program finds the happiness activities that fit you , trains them at the right dose, and measures whether they work.

The takeaway

It's not the new job, the apartment, or the partner that makes you lastingly happier – we adapt to almost everything. What lasts is only what we do regularly. And that can be trained.

Your happiness baseline

Person-Activity-Fit

Most happiness advice fails because it recommends the same thing to everyone. Lyubomirsky's key insight: an activity only works if it fits your personality, your values, and your life. Rate five statements for each of the 12 activities (1 = not at all true, 7 = completely true).
