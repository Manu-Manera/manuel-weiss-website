Speak honestly without hurting

Nonviolent Communication leads in four steps from blame to connection: observation, feeling, need, request. Each step has typical traps – this tool spots them in your text before your counterpart does.

ObservationFeelingNeedRequestEmpathy

1 · The situation

👁️ Observation

What would a camera record? No interpretation, no generalisation – only what was seen or heard.

Judgement → observation
You are always late.This week you arrived three times after the agreed time.
You never listen to me.While I was talking, you looked at your phone.
That was disrespectful.You interrupted me before I had finished the sentence.

🫀 Feeling

What do you feel – in your body, not in your judgement? “I feel ignored” is not a feeling but an interpretation of what the other person does.

6

💎 Need

Feelings are messengers: pleasant ones show met needs, unpleasant ones show unmet needs. Which one is behind your feeling?

And the other side?

Which need did your counterpart presumably meet with their behaviour? That is the core of empathy – and defuses almost every conflict.

🙏 Request

Concrete, positive, doable now. And: a request where a no is not okay is a demand.

The demand test

What happens inside you if the answer is “no”?
