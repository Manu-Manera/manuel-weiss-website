Is your life standing on solid pillars?

Hilarion Petzold describes identity as a roof on five pillars: body, relationships, work, security, meaning. If one breaks, the others carry – if they are strong enough. Put everything on one pillar and you fall with it. You rate each pillar, spot concentration risk, understand the interactions and deliberately strengthen what wobbles.
