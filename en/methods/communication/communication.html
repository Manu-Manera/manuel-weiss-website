What's heard isn't what's meant

Every message has four sides – factual, self-disclosure, relationship, appeal – and everyone listens with four ears. Misunderstandings arise when the side that was meant isn't the side that was heard. Here you break down a real situation, find the point where it tipped, get to know your ear profile and formulate what you actually want to say.
