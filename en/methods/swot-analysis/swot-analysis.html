Clarity through four fields

SWOT separates what lies in you (strengths, weaknesses) from what comes from outside (opportunities, threats). Done right it does not end at four lists, but at strategies: Which strength uses which opportunity? Which weakness makes which threat dangerous? And what do you do first?
