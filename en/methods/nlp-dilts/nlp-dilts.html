Change on every level

Robert Dilts describes six levels – from environment to purpose. A change on a higher level changes everything below; the reverse is not true. You take your topic up, get the resource at the top and climb back down with it. Then you see where it sticks – and where the lever is.

2 · Ascent – how is it today?

Go from the bottom up. Each level gets an honest answer for the current state. Once you are at the top, the world usually looks different.

3 · Descent – with the resource from above

Take with you what you found at purpose and identity. Go from the top down and ask on each level: how does this change if I act from there?

4 · Coherence

Do the levels fit together? A behavior that contradicts identity costs energy – every day. Rate how well each level fits the rest.
