Your character strengths

Peterson and Seligman described 24 character strengths that are valued in all cultures – organised into six virtues. Your “signature strengths” are the three to five that feel like you, give you energy and that you like to use. Whoever uses a signature strength in a new way for a week is demonstrably more satisfied – even months later.
