What gets you out of bed in the morning?

Ikigai (生き甲斐) is what makes your life worth living – the place where what you love, what you are good at, what the world needs and what you can be paid for overlap. You collect concrete keywords in each circle; the synthesis shows you where they converge.

The model

Four questions, four circles. Every pairwise overlap has a name – and shows what is missing if you only stand there.

Where you stand

Before you go into the circles: an honest look at values, formative experiences, dreams and fears. There are no wrong answers – keywords are enough.

Where do your keywords converge?

Every keyword you entered in a circle shows up here. Mark which other circles it also belongs to. The more circles, the closer to your ikigai.

Assignment matrix

Tap the circles to assign a keyword. Keywords with three circles are “almost there” – the fourth column shows you the matching question.

Your ikigai in one sentence

Build a first sentence from your keywords – then rephrase it in your own words.

Be specific: Where do you wake up, what do you do in the morning, who do you talk to, how does the day end?

From sentence to action

Ikigai is not a goal but a direction. Plan in three horizons – and start with one step that fits this week.
