Change is not a switch – it is a staircase

Prochaska & DiClemente showed: people change in stages – from “not an issue” via “I’m considering” and “I’m preparing” to “I’m doing it” and “I’m sticking with it”. Choose the step that fits your stage and you move forward. Skip a stage and you stall. Here you find out where you stand.
