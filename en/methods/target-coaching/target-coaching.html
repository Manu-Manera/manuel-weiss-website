Coach yourself with GROW

GROW is the most widely used coaching model: Goal – Reality – Options – Will. You clarify where you want to go, where you stand, which paths exist and what you will actually do. With goal scale, options matrix and check-ins to keep going.

The goal scale

10 = goal fully achieved. Describe what 10 looks like – and what a realistic "good enough" would be.

What holds you back?

Quantity first, then quality. Collect at least five – including ones you would never do. Then rate impact and effort.
