Paint your future

A vision board is not a wish list. It's a picture of what your life looks like if you hold the course – so concrete that your brain starts spotting opportunities. You write the big picture, fill in the areas of life, arrange the board and translate the most important building blocks into first steps.

Big picture10 areas of lifeVisual boardFocus → ActionRitual

1 · The big picture

Write in the present tense, as if it were already true. Not “I will”, but “I am”, “I have”, “I live”.

2 · Areas of life

One building block per area is enough. Image (emoji), one sentence in the present tense, importance. Areas you don't care about can stay empty.

3 · Your board

Tap up to three building blocks that make the biggest difference – your focus for the coming months.

4 · From picture to action

Visions without steps are daydreams. For each focus block: how will you know it's coming true – and what will you do in the next seven days?
