A goal that really pulls

Many goals fail because they’re unclear, negated, or set by others. The NLP well-formedness criteria make your goal precise, self-directed, and congruent. The check below walks you through every step.

PositiveSelf-initiatedSensory-specificMeta-goalEcological

1 · Phrased positively

Describe what you want – not what you no longer want. The brain cannot think a negation as an image.

Is it a goal or a problem?

2 · Self-initiative

A well-formed outcome starts and sustains itself through you. You can’t make what others do into a goal – but you can make how you behave into one.

Everything here is not part of your goal, but a framework condition.

Context

Where, when and with whom do you want the goal – and where expressly not?

3 · Evidence – how do you recognize it?

Make it tangible for your senses. Imagine it’s achieved – you’re standing in the middle of it.

The outsider test

4 · Meta-goal

Behind every goal is a larger one. “What do you want that for?” – and then again. The meta-goal is the real engine; there are often several paths there.

Secondary gain

The current state has advantages too – otherwise you’d have changed it long ago. What would you lose if you reach the goal?

5 · Resources

What do you need – and what of that do you already have?

Ecology check

Does the goal fit your life as a whole? Honest answers prevent later self-sabotage.
