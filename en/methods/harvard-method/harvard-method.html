Hard on the issue, soft on the people

The Harvard method replaces haggling over positions with the search for interests. Four principles: separate the people from the problem, interests instead of positions, options for mutual gain, objective criteria – plus your BATNA as a safety net.

The negotiation

1 · Separate the people from the problem

What is the issue – and what is relationship, emotion, history? Both are real, but they are treated separately.

2 · Interests instead of positions

A position is what someone demands. An interest is why. Behind incompatible positions there are often compatible interests.
