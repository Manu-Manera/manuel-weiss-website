What others see – and you don’t

The Johari window by Luft and Ingham divides you into four areas: what you and others know (open), what only you know (hidden), what only others see (blind spot) and what nobody knows (unknown). Here you choose your self-image, enter real views from others – ideally from several people – and see where your window grows when you ask more and show more.
