Build the solution, not the problem

Solution-focused brief coaching turns the gaze: instead of figuring out why something doesn’t work, you look for when it already did – and what you contributed. Miracle question, scaling and exceptions lead you to the next doable step.

Your concern

First describe freely what’s on your mind. Then we flip it.

From problem to goal

The method’s most important question: What do you want instead? A goal is something you tust and that anwesend is – not the absence of something.

Three check questions

The miracle question

Imagine: tonight, while you sleep, a miracle happens – and your concern is resolved. You don’t know it, though, because you were asleep. What’s the first thing you notice tomorrow morning that tells you the miracle has happened?

Stay concrete and observable: not “I am more relaxed”, but “I drink my coffee at the window before I look at my phone”.

Your miracle signs

Condense the description into concrete, observable signs. They’ll later become your measuring tool.

Scaling

0 = the day you decided to change something. 10 = the miracle has fully happened.

Questions about the scale

Confidence & importance

Two side scales that often reveal more than the main scale.

Exceptions

No problem is always equally strong. When was it last smaller, shorter, or completely gone – even for just an hour? Every exception is a piece of solution that already exists.

What you learn from this

Compliments to yourself

In solution-focused work there are compliments after every session – honest and concrete. Do it for yourself: What have you already done right, even though the problem is still there?

Coping questions

For phases when the scale is low.

The classic observation task after the first session – it directs attention to what works.

Your next small step

Not the solution – just the next point on the scale. Small enough that you’ll definitely take it.

Scale history

Come back in a few days and set the scale again. “What has gotten better?” is the opening question of every follow-up session.
