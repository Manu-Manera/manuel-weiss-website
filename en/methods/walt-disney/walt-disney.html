Three roles, one strong plan

Walt Disney split three ways of thinking into three rooms: The Dreamer may do anything, the Realist turns them into plans, the Critic finds the gaps. The split is what matters – anyone who dreams and criticizes at the same time gets neither good ideas nor good plans.

The three rooms

💭DreamerEverything is possible. No objections, no “but”. What would be ideal?
🛠️RealistHow exactly? What is needed? Who, when, with what? No judgment.
🔍CriticWhat's missing? What risks? Ask questions – don't put it down.

💭 The Dreamer:

You are in the dream room. Money, time and skill are unlimited. Describe the ideal version – in images, not in plans. Every objection is only allowed with the critic.

Dream starters

The dream elements

Break the vision into individual building blocks. Each one gets a plan later.

🛠️ The Realist:

You are in the planning room. The dreams are set – don't question them, implement them. For each element: how, with what, with whom, by when?

Resources total

🔍 The Critic:

You are in the review room. The critic is not a naysayer, but quality assurance. He attacks the plans – never the dreamer. Good critique is a question: “What happens if …?”
